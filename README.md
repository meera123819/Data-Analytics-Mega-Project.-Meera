District wise Crime Against Women Analysis
##Project Overview- This project analysis district wise crimes against women in India using Data Analytics Techniques. The objective 
is to identify crime patterns, trends, correlations and regional variations different states and districts. Rape, Attempt to commit Rape, Kidnapping and
and Abduction, Dowry Death, Assault o Women with intent to Outrage Modesty, Insult to Modesty of Women, Cruelty by HUuband or Relatives
Importation of Girls, Abetment of suicide of women, Attempt to acid attacks, Cyber crimes against women and other crimes against women.
These are the crime related to women. 

##Dataset- District Wise Crime Against Women (2017 onwards)

##Tasks Performed- Data Loading and Initial Overview, Data Cleaning and Preprocessing, Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
Correlation Analysis, Data visualization, Insight Generation.

##Tools Used- Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Jupyter Notebook.

##Key Findings

- Domestic violence related crimes are among the most reported categories.
- Crime rates vary significantly across districts and states,
- Certain districts show exceptionally high crime counts.
- Positive correlations exist between several crime categories.
- Regional disparities are evident in crime distribution.
